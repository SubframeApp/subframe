Pages are used to design screens of your product using your design system.

## Creating a page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/pages/new-page.png" className="w-[760px]" alt="A canvas heading in the Pages view with Open prototype, New page, and Open canvas buttons" />
</Frame>

In the **Pages** view, click **New page** next to the canvas you want to add it to. The blank page opens in the editor.

To generate a page design from a prompt, use [Agent chat](/design/ask-ai/prompt-to-design).

## Renaming a page

Double-click a page name in the pages sidebar or on the canvas to rename it. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to confirm or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel.

Page names can contain any characters, including spaces and symbols. When you [export a page](/design/code-mode/overview#inspecting-page-code), Subframe normalizes the name into a valid component name in the generated code.

## Duplicating a page

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/pages/duplicate-page.png" className="w-[160px]" alt="A page's menu with Duplicate highlighted between Move to canvas, Copy as PNG, and Delete" />
</Frame>

1. Right-click a page or click <Icon icon="ellipsis" size={16} />
2. Select **Duplicate**

## Sorting pages

Use the sort control at the top of the **Pages** view to change how pages are ordered:

- **Group by canvas** — Pages organized under their [canvases](/design/canvases) (default)
- **Recently edited** — Most recently edited first
- **Recently created** — Most recently created first
- **A-Z** — Alphabetical by name

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How do I design page states?">

For page states like loading, empty, and error states, we recommend creating a separate page for each state. You can easily duplicate the page for this. Organize the screens together on a [canvas](/design/canvases) and use [annotations](/design/prototype-mode/annotations) to describe how the screens are connected during handoff.
