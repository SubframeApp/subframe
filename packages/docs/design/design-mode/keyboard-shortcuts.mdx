## Searching your project

Press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> to search your project. You can also select **Search project...** in the project menu.

- Create a canvas, page, component, snippet, or doc
- Jump to a canvas, page, component, snippet, or doc
- Open a project section, such as **Theme** or **Icons**, a theme section such as **Typography**, or a settings page such as **Billing**
- Switch to another of your projects

## Zooming in and out

These shortcuts work on canvases and in the editor:

| Action                     | Mac                                                   | Windows and Linux                                      |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Zoom in                    | <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>=</kbd>, or <kbd>=</kbd>        | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>=</kbd>, or <kbd>=</kbd>        |
| Zoom out                   | <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>-</kbd>, or <kbd>-</kbd>        | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>-</kbd>, or <kbd>-</kbd>        |
| Zoom in on a spot or area  | Hold <kbd>Z</kbd>, then click or drag a box           | Hold <kbd>Z</kbd>, then click or drag a box            |
| Zoom out from a spot       | Hold <kbd>Option</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>, then click     | Hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>, then click         |

Dragging a box while holding <kbd>Z</kbd> zooms in to fit that area. **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, and **Zoom to 100%** are also in the project menu.

## Step back to where you came from

Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> twice to navigate back up your editing history:

- From a component or snippet that you opened from another design, return to that previous design
- From a page on a canvas, return to the canvas

In the page editor, the canvas pulses on the first press as visual feedback.
