---
title: Sharing designs
description: Invite teammates or publish a preview anyone can view.
---

Everyone on your team can access the team's projects. Use **Share → Copy link** to send a design to a teammate.

## Inviting teammates

1. Click **Share** at the top of the Inspector panel
2. In the **Share** tab, enter email addresses separated by commas
3. Select a role and click **Invite**

The recipients receive an email invitation. See [team roles](/admin/managing-team#choosing-a-role) for access and billing details.

## Publishing a public preview

1. Open the page, component, snippet, or layout in its editor
2. Click **Share**, then open the **Publish** tab
3. Click **Publish page** (or the button for the type of design you're sharing)
4. Click **Copy public link**

Anyone with the public link can view the preview without signing in. On a canvas, **Publish** shares its generated [prototype](/design/prototype-mode/overview#sharing-prototypes). Open the individual page editor to publish just that page.

## Stopping public sharing

Open **Share → Publish**, click **Can view**, and select **Stop sharing**. Team members retain their access through the project.
