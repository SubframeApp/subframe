<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the desktop app">
    [Download Subframe for macOS ↗](https://subframe.com/downloads) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    [Choose an agent](/agent/connecting-an-agent): use Subframe AI credits or connect your Claude Code or Codex subscription.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of agent connection */}

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your codebase">
    [Select your local repository](/agent/connecting-your-codebase). The agent reads and edits files in this folder.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of codebase connection */}

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your app">
    [Start your development server and open your app](/browser/previewing-your-local-app) from a new tab.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of detected servers on the new tab */}

  </Step>
  <Step title="Make design changes">
    [Use **Inspect**](/browser/making-live-edits) to edit an element on the live page, or [capture the page](/browser/capturing-as-designs) to explore bigger changes on the canvas.

    {/* TODO: Add video of inspect + capture */}

  </Step>
  <Step title="Implement and review">
    For browser edits, click **Send** to ask the agent to apply them to your code. For a canvas design, [ask the agent to implement the page](/agent/making-code-changes). Review the result in your running app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
