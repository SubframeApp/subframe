Capture turns what's in your browser into an editable design. Instead of recreating a page by hand, start from the real one and design on top of it. This works on any website, not just your app.

## Capturing a page

Here's how to capture a full page:

1. Open the page in a browser tab
2. Click **Capture**

Subframe converts the live page into an editable design and copies it to your clipboard. Then place it where you want it:

- Click **Add to flow** in the confirmation toast to put it on a canvas right away
- Or paste it into any flow or agent chat

## Capturing a selection

To capture part of a page instead:

1. Click **Inspect** and select an element
2. Copy it with <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd> or **Copy selection**
3. Paste it into a flow or agent chat
