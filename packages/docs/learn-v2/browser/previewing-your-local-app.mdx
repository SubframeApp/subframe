Open your app in a browser tab to start [editing it live](/learn-v2/browser/making-live-edits) or [capturing it as a design](/learn-v2/browser/capturing-as-designs).

Here's how to open your app in a browser tab:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your codebase">
    Point Subframe at your local repository so it knows where your app lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your dev server">
    Ask the agent to start it for you. You don't have to start it through Subframe: a server you start from the terminal works just as well.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open localhost">
    Your running server appears under **Detected servers** on the new tab page. Click it to open your app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Opening any URL

You can also enter a URL directly, whether that's `localhost:3000` or any website. Browser tabs work like the browser you're used to: press <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>T</kbd> for a new tab and <kbd>Cmd</kbd> + <kbd>W</kbd> to close one.

<Note>If your server doesn't appear under **Detected servers**, see [Troubleshooting](/learn-v2/browser/troubleshooting).</Note>
