Here's how to connect your agent and codebase, then make a design change to your live app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the desktop app">
    Download Subframe for macOS at [subframe.com/downloads ↗](https://subframe.com/downloads) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    Connect Claude Code or Codex using your existing subscription.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of agent connection */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your codebase">
    Point Subframe at your local repository. Your code stays on your machine.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of codebase connection */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your app">
    Start your dev server, then open it from the new tab. Subframe detects servers running on localhost automatically.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of detected servers on the new tab */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make design changes">
    Use **Inspect** to select and edit any element on the live page, or **Capture** the page onto the canvas to explore bigger changes.

    {/* TODO: Add video of inspect + capture */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship with the agent">
    Paste your design or selection into chat and ask the agent to implement it in your codebase.
  </Step>
</Steps>
