Here's how to connect your agent and codebase, then make a design change to your live app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the desktop app">
    [Download Subframe for macOS ↗](https://subframe.com/downloads) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your agent">
    [Connect Claude Code or Codex](/learn-v2/agent/connecting-an-agent) using your existing subscription.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of agent connection */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your codebase">
    [Select your local repository](/learn-v2/agent/connecting-your-codebase). Your code stays on your machine.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of codebase connection */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your app">
    [Start your development server and open your app](/learn-v2/browser/previewing-your-local-app) from a new tab.

    {/* TODO: Add screenshot of detected servers on the new tab */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make design changes">
    [Use **Inspect**](/learn-v2/browser/making-live-edits) to edit an element on the live page, or [capture the page](/learn-v2/browser/capturing-as-designs) to explore bigger changes on the canvas.

    {/* TODO: Add video of inspect + capture */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship with the agent">
    [Ask the agent to implement your design](/learn-v2/agent/making-code-changes) in your codebase. Agent chat automatically includes the design or browser page you're viewing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
